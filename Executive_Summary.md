# Executive Summary: Project AURA

## Objective
Give merchandising leaders one measurement framework for promotions, pricing, customers and inventory productivity across four boxes, using synthetic data that mirrors the structure of a fashion retailer.

## Findings
1. **Markdown depth costs margin faster than volume repays it.** Gross margin per order falls from $177 at full price to $124 at 1-20% off and $79 at 21-30% off in full-line boxes. At 41-50% off in off-price boxes, margin is 9.6% of net revenue.
2. **Events create traffic, not margin.** The Anniversary Sale lifted orders by about 12 per channel-day (95% CI 10.1 to 14.2) and net revenue by $2.7k per channel-day, with a margin effect near zero.
3. **Rack events cannibalize full-price digital.** Nordstrom.com loses about 3.8 orders per day during Clear the Rack events (95% CI 1.3 to 6.4), which is the main reason the events lose margin overall.
4. **Returns reshape channel quality.** Nordstrom.com earns 47.4% gross margin but 37.1% after returns, below Nordstrom Stores at 43.4%.
5. **A small segment carries most of the revenue.** Core loyalists are 47% of buying customers and 81.5% of revenue. Deal seekers are 20% of customers and 6.6% of revenue.
6. **Inventory productivity differs by category.** Annualized GMROI is 4.20 for Beauty and Fragrance and 2.33 for Handbags and Accessories.

## Recommendation
Test a Rack-only audience for Clear the Rack promotions with a Nordstrom.com holdout, and cap full-line markdowns at 20% until the test reports. See Executive_Memo.md.

## Confidence and limits
Results describe synthetic data with known built-in effects. They demonstrate method, not any real retailer's performance.
