# Memo: Clear the Rack promotions

**Decision requested:** Approve a controlled test of Rack-only promotion targeting.

**Evidence (five events, synthetic data)**
- Rack channels: +$119k net revenue, -$15k gross margin.
- Nordstrom.com: -$102k net revenue, -$48k gross margin, -212 orders.
- Total: +$17k net revenue, -$64k gross margin.

**Options**
| Option | Revenue effect | Gross margin effect | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Keep current events | +$17k | -$64k | Continued cannibalization |
| B. Rack-only targeting, Nordstrom.com holdout | Up to +$119k | Up to -$15k if cannibalization is removed | Spillover to other audiences may remain |
| C. Pause Clear the Rack | $0 | $0 | Loses Rack traffic and clearance of aged stock |

**Recommendation:** Option B as a test on the next event, with Nordstrom Stores as the comparison group. Success means Nordstrom.com orders stay within 5% of baseline and event margin is no worse than -$3k per event.

**Caveats:** Estimates come from simulated data. Option B's upper bound assumes cannibalization can be fully removed, which a real test must confirm.
