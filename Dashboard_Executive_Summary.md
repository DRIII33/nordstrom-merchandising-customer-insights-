# Looker Studio Dashboard
Five pages: Executive overview, Promotion effectiveness, Customer insights, Category and inventory health, Data trust.

## KPIs and definitions
- Net Revenue, Gross Margin $, Gross Margin % = margin / net revenue
- AOV = net revenue / orders
- GMROI (annualized) = gross margin / average inventory at cost x 12
- Return-Adjusted Margin % = margin after reversing returned orders / net revenue
- Promo Revenue Share = promotional net revenue / net revenue
- Category Health Index (0-100) = 35% GMROI + 25% return-adjusted margin + 20% 30-day repeat rate + 20% low promo dependence, each percentile-ranked

All ratios are computed from summed numerators and denominators, never averaged.
